Document Flow — Start to Finish

Phase 1 — Onboard the Carrier

Between you and the carrier
Carrier Onboarding Form You → Carrier
You send it; carrier fills in their company, equipment, insurance, and factoring info.
Dispatch Service Agreement You → Carrier
You send it; carrier signs. Sets your 7%, per-load billing, and appoints you as their agent.
Letter of Authorization You → Carrier
Carrier signs, authorizing you to act as their dispatch agent with brokers.
Carrier's documents Carrier → You
Collect: MC/DOT authority, certificate of insurance (COI), W-9, and factoring Notice of Assignment (if they factor).

Phase 2 — Set Up With a Broker

Between you and the broker, on the carrier's behalf
Broker's carrier setup packet Broker → You, you fill
The broker sends their own packet; you complete it using the carrier's info.
Carrier Information Sheet + docs You → Broker
Send the carrier's profile along with their authority, COI, and W-9.
Broker approval Broker → You
Broker approves the carrier and issues a carrier code. Now you can book loads.

Phase 3 — Book a Load

Between you and the broker
Rate Confirmation Broker issues, carrier accepts
Broker sends the rate con (rate, lane, dates). You review it; the carrier accepts.
Load details You → Driver
Pass pickup/delivery, appointments, directions, and the broker's load number to the driver.

Phase 4 — Run the Load

Driver, on the road
Permits (oversize loads)
Obtained before pickup for oversize/overweight freight.
Bill of Lading (BOL) Shipper → Driver
Shipper issues at pickup; driver signs and carries it.
Proof of Delivery (POD) Signed at delivery
Receiver signs at delivery; driver keeps it. This proves the load delivered.

Phase 5 — Get Paid

Money flows — kept separate to stay a dispatcher, not a broker
Carrier invoices broker Carrier → Broker
Carrier bills the broker with the BOL/POD. Broker pays the carrier directly.
You invoice the carrier You → Carrier
You bill the carrier your 7% of gross linehaul, per load, on delivery — separate from the freight payment.
You → you produce/send → You you receive/collect Fill/forward broker's doc, you handle
These templates are starting points. Have a transportation attorney review the Dispatch Service Agreement before signing a carrier. Fill and sign through DocuSign, then upload the completed documents to the load's Documents tab so every load has its paperwork on file.
