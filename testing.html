Training Walkthrough

Run one practice load through the system from fleet setup and sourcing until it becomes a load in All Loads.

1. Fleet Setup
Create the training carrier, driver, tractor and trailer. These resources will later be assigned to the test load.
Open Fleet Setup →
2. Sourcing
Create a training opportunity from a broker load. Enter the broker, origin, destination, freight, equipment, dimensions, weight and rate.
Open Sourcing →
3. Opportunity
Review the sourced load and move it forward when the load is accepted or booked. The goal is to carry the same information forward instead of re-entering it.
Opportunities →
4. Build the Load
Convert the opportunity into an operational load. Confirm the carrier, driver, truck, trailer and freight information carried forward correctly.
New Load →
5. All Loads
Final checkpoint: confirm the training load appears in All Loads with the correct broker, freight, route, rate and fleet assignment.
View All Loads →
TT Goal: Start with training fleet records, source a broker opportunity, convert it into a real load workflow, and successfully push that same load into All Loads without losing its information.
